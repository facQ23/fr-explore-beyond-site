FR Explore Beyond AI

A personal application for reviewing YouTube channel analytics and delivering scheduled reports to a Telegram account.

What it does

With the channel owner's permission, it reads YouTube channel information and analytics through Google's OAuth services. It generates performance summaries and can deliver them through Telegram. The application is under development and is not offered as a public service.

Permissions

Google sign-in requests read-only YouTube account and YouTube Analytics access. It does not request access to upload or delete videos.
